THE LITTLE DETAILS

A few things
to know.

What do I need to get started?+

A supported camera device, a second device to view it, and MirrorMirror on both. The current iPhone and iPad build requires iOS or iPadOS 18.2 or later. Full beta compatibility details will accompany the invitation.

Does the camera keep running when I lock my iPhone?+

Keep MirrorMirror open in camera mode on an iPhone or iPad. Locking the device or switching away can interrupt camera capture. Use the app’s dimmed camera screen when you want the display to stay dark.

Where are recordings stored?+

On the camera device. Choose a storage cap and recording mode. Older footage is removed when the storage cap is reached, so export any clips you want to keep.

Can I connect from another network?+

MirrorMirror supports remote connection setup through iCloud. Some carrier and office networks restrict direct connections and may require an optional relay. Connection behavior depends on the networks at both ends.

How does Apple Watch viewing work?+

Live pictures and voice are relayed through your paired iPhone. If that connection is unavailable, the Watch can use encrypted still images through iCloud. The Watch does not run an independent live video connection.

How can I try the beta?+

The public TestFlight invitation will be added here when it is ready. In the meantime, explore the product and its companion viewing experiences above.
