# MirrorMirror experience capture session

Use physical devices or official hardware imagery. Capture the running app directly; preserve its controls, content and aspect ratio. Scenario photographs sit outside the product captures. The current website includes genuine development captures, but companion setup/disconnected screens still need live replacements.

| Experience | Camera → viewer | Product capture | Physical scene |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nearby nursery check | Spare iPhone → paired iPhone relay → Watch | Camera list, live picture, listen, and return to list | Parent glancing at a real Watch while nearby; camera placement and nursery in a separate shot |
| Pet beside your work | Spare iPhone or iPad → Mac | Connected camera window beside another work window; talk-back | Person working at a real Mac with pet resting in the camera’s room |
| Several rooms at a desk | Two iPhone/iPad cameras → Mac | Two connected camera windows or camera wall | Real desk and devices, consistent camera names and scenes |
| Reverse camera pairing | Mac camera → iPhone | Mac in camera mode and corresponding live iPhone viewer | Mac left at the chosen viewpoint; viewer in another room |
| Kitchen check-in | Spare iPhone → iPad | Connected live view with listen/talk controls | Real iPad on a kitchen counter; camera location in another shot |
| Sofa camera wall | Multiple iPhone/iPad cameras → Apple TV | Connected wall, remote focus, one full-screen camera | Real television and remote in a living room |
| Scenic window | iPhone or iPad pointed toward a view → Vision Pro | A connected camera window in visionOS; optional second camera window | Person wearing actual Vision Pro; corresponding real outdoor camera view photographed separately |

For each story, record a clean screenshot, a short screen recording of the action, a wide physical-device photograph and a close-up. Keep landscape and nursery shots separate from app screenshots until those scenes have actually streamed through the app. Vision Pro presents a camera video in a window; capture that actual experience.

Match camera names, recording states, times and source scenes throughout each story. Preserve original captures alongside optimized exports. Keep still-image fallback distinct from live Watch relay in the asset set. Review final companion availability and minimum OS versions against the beta build before publishing.
